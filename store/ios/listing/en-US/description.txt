Ortus Workspace is the iPhone app for Ortus, the workspace an organisation runs on its own servers: mail, chat, calendar, files, documents, tasks and approvals in one place, with nothing going to anyone else's cloud.

EVERYTHING STAYS WITH YOUR ORGANISATION
The app talks only to your organisation's Ortus server. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing is sent to the app's publisher.

MAIL
• Your inbox sorted into Important, Other and Feeds
• Threads, quick replies and new messages, with names suggested as you type
• See whether mail to people outside was delivered
• Sent, Archive and Trash

CHAT AND CALLS
• Your channels and direct messages
• Join a channel's call, even on networks that allow only web traffic

CALENDAR, FILES AND TASKS
• Your day and week, and invitations to answer
• Files and documents shared with you
• Approvals and signatures waiting for you, signed with your phone's own key

SIGN IN WITHOUT A PASSWORD
Scan the code from your welcome letter, or choose "Sign in on your phone" on the web. Your iPhone then becomes your key, protected by Face ID; there is no password to steal.

You need an account at an organisation that runs Ortus.
